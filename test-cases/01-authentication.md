Covers: `AuthController` (`/api/auth/login|register|refresh|forgot-password|reset-password|logout`), `JwtTokenProvider`, `JwtAuthFilter`, `LoginRateLimitFilter`, `AuthService.validatePasswordStrength`.

Roles: `ADMIN, DIRECTOR, MANAGER, ASSISTANT_MANAGER, HR, EMPLOYEE`.
